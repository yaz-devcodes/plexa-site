Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Plexa (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with our Atlassian Marketplace apps, including Plexa Markdown Editor for Confluence (the “Apps”).

1. Who we are

Plexa builds productivity apps for Atlassian Cloud products. Our Apps run on the Atlassian Forge platform.

Contact for privacy questions: yaz-devcodes@proton.me

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • Installation and use of our Apps on Atlassian Cloud sites
  • Information we receive through Atlassian when you install or use an App
  • Communications you send us for support or privacy requests

It does not cover Atlassian’s own products or websites. Atlassian’s processing is described in Atlassian’s privacy documentation.

3. How our Apps process data

Our Apps are designed to run on Atlassian Forge. For Apps that qualify for Runs on Atlassian, customer content and App data stay within Atlassian’s cloud infrastructure and are not sent to Plexa-operated servers.

In particular, for Plexa Markdown Editor:

  • Markdown content you write in the macro is stored as part of your Confluence page content in your Atlassian site
  • The App does not use external remotes or egress to Plexa infrastructure for that content (unless we change this later and update this policy and the Marketplace Privacy & Security information)
  • We do not operate a separate database that stores your Confluence page content for this App

4. Information we may receive

Depending on how Atlassian exposes App lifecycle and support data, we may receive:

  • Installation metadata (for example, that an App was installed on a site, licensing/entitlement status when Marketplace licensing applies)
  • Limited contact details you provide when you email us for support
  • Diagnostic details you voluntarily include in a support request (for example screenshots or descriptions of an issue)

We do not use the Apps to sell personal data. We do not use third-party advertising trackers inside the Apps.

5. How we use information

We use information only to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Apps
  • Respond to support and privacy requests
  • Meet legal, security, and Marketplace obligations
  • Communicate about material changes to the Apps or this policy when required

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only:

  • With Atlassian, as required to operate Forge / Marketplace apps
  • With service providers who help us operate our business (for example email), under appropriate terms
  • When required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and security

7. Retention

Confluence content remains under your organisation’s Atlassian site retention and admin controls. Support emails and related correspondence are retained only as long as needed to resolve requests and meet legal obligations, then deleted or anonymised.

8. Security

We rely on Atlassian Forge security controls for App runtime and hosting. Access to our own inboxes and accounts used for support is limited to authorised people.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. For App content stored in Confluence, your site admins typically control access through Atlassian product permissions.

To make a privacy request related to information we hold directly (for example support email history), contact yaz-devcodes@proton.me.

10. Children

The Apps are intended for business use on Atlassian Cloud and are not directed at children.

11. International processing

Atlassian may process data in regions according to your site’s configuration and Atlassian’s cloud infrastructure. Where we process support communications, that may occur in the region where our mail provider and team operate.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Apps evolve. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, when changes are material, take additional steps as required (including updating Marketplace listing information).
